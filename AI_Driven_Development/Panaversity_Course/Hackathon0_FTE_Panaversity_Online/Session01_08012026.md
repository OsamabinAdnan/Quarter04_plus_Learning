**Is General Agent is on Local or have we other options??**
A General Agent is not limited to local. The most common and recommended pattern is a hybrid setup where the agent orchestration runs locally/on your server, but uses cloud-based LLMs for the AI reasoning.

## General Agent vs Custom Agent — Brief Explanation

### 🌐 General Agents
A **General Agent** is a broad-purpose AI assistant that can handle a wide variety of tasks without being tailored to a specific domain.

* **Examples:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot
* **Characteristics:**
    * Can answer questions, write code, summarize text, translate, etc.
    * **Not specialized** — it knows a little about everything.
    * Uses a large general-purpose LLM as its brain.
    * No deep knowledge of your specific business data or workflows.
* **Analogy:** A general-purpose employee who can do many things but isn't an expert in any one area.

### 🎯 Custom Agents
A **Custom Agent** is purpose-built for a specific task, role, or domain within your company.

* **Examples:** A customer support bot for your product, an HR agent that answers leave policy questions, a code reviewer for your codebase.
* **Characteristics:**
    * **Specialized** — trained/configured for a narrow set of tasks.
    * Has access to your data (via RAG, tools, APIs, databases).
    * Uses custom instructions, tools, and workflows tailored to your needs.
    * Much more reliable and accurate for its specific domain.
* **Analogy:** A specialist employee hired for a specific role (e.g., accountant, support rep, data analyst).

## Hackathon Scope & Tiered Deliverables

### Architecture & Tech Stack:
The proposed stack is robust, privacy-focused, and clever:

* **The Brain:** Claude Code acts as the reasoning engine. We add the Ralph Wiggum Stop hook to let the agent continuously iterate until the assigned task is complete.
* **The Memory/GUI:** Obsidian (local Markdown) is used as the dashboard, keeping data local and accessible.
* **The Senses (Watchers):** Lightweight Python scripts monitor Gmail, WhatsApp, and filesystems to trigger the AI.
* **The Hands (MCP):** Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers handle external actions like sending emails or clicking buttons.
