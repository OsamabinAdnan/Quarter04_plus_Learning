# Docker 101:

Watch the [following video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rIrNIzy6U_g) 3 times and understand all the concepts of Docker.

If you goal is to ship software in the real world, one of the most powerful concept to understand is the **Containerization**. When developing locally it solves the age-old problem of `it works on my machine` and when deploying on cloud it solves the age-old problem of `this architecture doesnt scale`.

We'll unlock the power inside this container by learning 101 different concepts and terms related to `Computer Science`, `Cloud` and `Docker`.
